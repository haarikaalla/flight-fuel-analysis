## Operating checklist

- Maintain a dated progress diary; record real work and actual guide feedback, and obtain confirmation of its current format and submission channel.
- Draft the Phase 1 report around the four rubric categories, with a report schedule and justified assumptions.
- Save reproducible figures and explain their meaning and limitations for the end-term presentation and viva.
- Capture each member's contribution if this is a group project; each student's required individual report should follow guide instructions.
- Before final submission, check current deadlines, official forms, filename convention, final-report length, plagiarism process and whether a paper communication is expected.
