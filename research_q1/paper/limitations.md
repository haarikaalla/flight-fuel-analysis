# Evidence boundary and threats to validity

- Fuel labels are differences between crowdsourced fuel-on-board reports, not
  direct engine fuel-flow measurements. Unit inference was physics-assisted and
  residual label error remains.
- PRC 2025 covers April–October 2025 and anonymizes airlines. It cannot alone
  establish multi-year, operator-level or global external validity.
- Receiver availability and ACARS equipage create selection bias. ADS-B
  missingness is not random.
- Type-level fitted mass and TSFC are approximations. They are neither measured
  take-off mass nor identified engine parameters.
- Aircraft×phase conditional coverage applies only to pre-specified cells with
  enough calibration data; hierarchical fallback weakens cell-specific claims.
- Estimated density ratios can be unstable. Weighted conformal results must be
  accompanied by clipping and effective-sample-size sensitivity.
- October 2025 was previously examined in the inherited project, so new analyses
  on it are exploratory rather than untouched confirmation.
- Operator and aircraft-family holdouts require legitimate identifiers and
  mappings. They must not be reconstructed from anonymized data.
- A dashboard is a communication artifact, not evidence of statistical or
  operational validity.
- The upstream repository has no software license recorded in this fork; obtain
  the upstream author's permission before redistributing derivative software.
