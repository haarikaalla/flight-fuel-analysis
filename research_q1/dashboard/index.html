Research status

What the repository currently demonstrates

Development study

Implemented: physics residual model, aircraft-by-phase calibration, exact and robust risk-budget selection, grouped validation, seven real metadata baselines, route-cluster intervals, SHAP and refit ablations.

Measured: the metadata-only scores and figures above. The full PI-MCS system has no verified performance result yet.

Needed for a confirmatory Q1 submission: full trajectory/weather inputs, independent locked labels, multi-year or external validation, and matched-budget selector experiments.
