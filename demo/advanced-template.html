What is advanced—and what is not yet claimed

The added selector solves a mixed-integer minimum-risk problem under explicit overall, duration and aircraft coverage constraints. The replay exposes individual decisions, subgroup disparity and the full accuracy–coverage frontier. This is a stronger ML systems baseline than the original greedy rule.

Research boundary: this artifact still uses pre-scored historical intervals. A real live system requires rolling-window retraining, aircraft metadata resolution, calibrated uncertainty, drift monitoring, an approved live-data source and an independently frozen evaluation period.
