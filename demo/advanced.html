System methods and data status

A layered evaluation workflow makes the model, decision rule and uncertainty behavior independently inspectable.

1

Fuel estimation

The existing trajectory-and-aircraft feature model supplies interval fuel predictions.

2

Error ranking

A separate risk score estimates which predictions are likely to be easier or harder.

3

Uncertainty calibration

Five-fold isotonic calibration and scaled conformal quantiles produce held-out diagnostics.

4

Coverage optimization

A HiGHS mixed-integer solver minimizes predicted risk under overall and subgroup access constraints.

Evaluation scope: the dashboard contains pre-scored historical benchmark intervals and works fully offline. The replay simulates sequential arrival for inspection; a live telemetry connector is not enabled. Reported uncertainty is cross-fitted on this benchmark and should be confirmed on a later untouched period before operational use.
